Additional Services

Puppy Starter Course

Foundational 6-week training program designed for puppies aged 8 to 16 weeks to build core obedience, confidence, and positive socialization skills.

Good Manners Group Class

Six weekly sessions focused on foundational household cues including sit, down, stay, recall, and loose-leash walking. Emphasizes building handler-dog focus in the presence of mild-to-moderate group distractions.

One-on-One Behavior Coaching

Behavior-focused training tailored to your dog’s individual needs. We work to understand the factors influencing behavior and develop practical, personalized strategies to address challenges, build confidence, and support lasting progress in everyday life. Behavior-focused training includes reactivity, fear, and anxiety.
