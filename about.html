Ary, Head Trainer, MPH

Hi, I’m Ary, owner and head trainer at The Canine Theory!

I have a bachelor’s degree in biology with a focus in pre-veterinary medicine, and a master’s degree in public health. For 12 years I’ve worked with all kinds of animals, but dogs have always been my focus. I’ve worked in a veterinary hospital, as a doggy daycare attendant and trainer, and as a shelter volunteer. That range gave me a well-rounded understanding of how dogs think, behave, and learn, and of what actually works in everyday life.

The Canine Theory incorporates science-based learning theory and the individual personality, genetics, and needs of your dog. No two dogs are the same, so no two training plans are either. I focus on clear communication, structured guidance, and purposeful plans that close the gap between you and your dog and build confidence on both ends of the leash.

Whether you’re trying to survive the puppy stage, untangling complex behavior issues, or simply hoping to understand your best friend a little better, I’ll give you a step-by-step roadmap to get there. Together we’ll turn chaos into confidence and a deeper connection.

Because training is more than sit and stay. It’s about communication, connection, and confidence.
